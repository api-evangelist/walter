# Walter

> Walter is a San Francisco-based Y Combinator (Spring 2026) startup building an AI employee for the manufacturing back office. It logs into the enterprise systems a company already runs on — SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Teams, Outlook and Gmail — the way a new hire would, and takes over manual paperwork: reading sales orders and purchase orders from emails and PDFs, capturing and validating the data, cross-checking it against the ERP, ordering from suppliers and catching pricing errors before they ship. Its purpose-built document-understanding model supports 270+ languages and reaches 90%+ accuracy after minimal training, cutting an order that took 15 minutes down to about one.

Walter has no public API, developer portal, SDKs, or API documentation as of 2026-07-21 — it deliberately requires no integration project, operating the same screens its customers' teams already use.
